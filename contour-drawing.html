Draw the contour lines

The dots below are spot elevations measured in the field, in feet. Connect points of equal elevation or estimate between points to draw each contour line, the 240 ft line is done for you as an example. Pick a value, turn on crossing hints if you want help finding where a line should pass, and click to place a point. The contour interval is 20 ft.
