Read the real quadrangle

This is an actual piece of the Valley Forge, PA 7.5-minute USGS quad. Everything you practiced on the drawn maps is here in real terrain: closed loops for hilltops, tightly-packed lines for steep ground, and contour V's where streams cut across. Work through the tasks on the right, two of them ask you to click directly on the map.

VALLEY FORGE, PA · 7.5′ QUAD SCALE 1:24,000 · C.I. 10 ft

2Click the highest ground on the map.

3Click the steepest slope on the map.

4Using the rule of V's, which way does Fishers Run flow?

5Estimate the total relief (highest index contour minus lowest), in feet.

6Connect it back: on the drawn maps, water collected in the low V-shaped valleys. Point to one place on this real map where you'd expect water to collect, and say how the contours tell you.
