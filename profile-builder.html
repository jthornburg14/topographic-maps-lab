Build the topographic profile

The map up top shows two hills with a transect line from A to B. Each dot is where a contour crosses that line. Your job: in the graph below, click on each dashed guide at the elevation of the contour it came from. The points connect into a side-view profile of the terrain. Contour interval is 40 ft; A and B sit at 400 ft to start you off.
